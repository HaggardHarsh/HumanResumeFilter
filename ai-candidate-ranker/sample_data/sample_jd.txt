Senior Backend Engineer — Cloud Platform Team

About the Role
We are looking for a Senior Backend Engineer to join our Cloud Platform team.
You will design, build, and operate the core services that power our
multi-tenant SaaS platform serving 2M+ active users. This is a high-impact
role where you will influence architecture decisions and mentor junior engineers.

Core Responsibilities
- Design and implement scalable microservices using Python and Go.
- Own the full lifecycle of backend services: design, development, testing,
  deployment, and monitoring.
- Lead the migration of legacy monolith components to event-driven architecture.
- Collaborate with product, data, and infrastructure teams to ship features
  that delight customers.
- Conduct code reviews and establish engineering best practices across the team.
- Participate in on-call rotations and lead incident response when necessary.
- Mentor junior and mid-level engineers through pairing and design reviews.

Required Skills
- 5+ years of professional backend development experience.
- Strong proficiency in Python; experience with Go is a significant plus.
- Deep understanding of distributed systems, REST/gRPC APIs, and message
  brokers (Kafka, RabbitMQ).
- Hands-on experience with cloud platforms (AWS or GCP): EC2/GKE, S3,
  Lambda/Cloud Functions, IAM.
- Proficiency with relational databases (PostgreSQL) and NoSQL stores (Redis,
  DynamoDB).
- Experience with containerization (Docker) and orchestration (Kubernetes).
- Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines (GitHub Actions, Jenkins, ArgoCD).

Preferred Skills
- Experience with infrastructure-as-code (Terraform, Pulumi).
- Contributions to open-source projects.
- Familiarity with observability stacks (Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry).
- Prior experience at a high-growth startup or scaling a product from
  thousands to millions of users.

Soft Skills & Behavioral Expectations
- Strong written and verbal communication skills.
- Ability to break down ambiguous problems into actionable engineering plans.
- A bias for action balanced with thoughtful design.
- Comfortable giving and receiving candid feedback.
- Demonstrated leadership: you don't need a title to lead.
